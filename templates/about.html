About Nur Al-Ilm

A platform dedicated to making authentic Islamic education accessible to every Muslim, everywhere.

Our Vision

Knowledge for Every Muslim

اقْرَأْ بِاسْمِ رَبِّكَ الَّذِي خَلَقَ

"Read in the name of your Lord who created." — Al-Alaq 96:1

Nur Al-Ilm (نور العلم — Light of Knowledge) was founded on the belief that authentic Islamic education should be accessible to every Muslim, regardless of where they live, what they earn, or how busy their schedule is.

We partner with qualified Islamic scholars to bring you structured, reliable, and engaging online courses — from foundational Aqeedah to advanced Fiqh studies.

Our Values

What We Stand For

Rooted in the classical tradition of Islamic scholarship while embracing modern learning methods.
